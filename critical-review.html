What these checks mean

Core Rules printed page 209: omitted counts mean 1, except bare penalties last until the wound is healed. Bleeding starts next round and continues until healed; must-parry without a penalty means 0. No-parry and must-parry cannot occur in the same result branch. Explicit counts and relevant permanent/until-healed conditions are preserved; bleeding stays ongoing. Supported equipment alternatives are checked separately and show notes: combat uses the foe’s actual equipment. Narrative conditions without parsed numeric consequences remain GM-managed. Recognized inactive-round/hour/day death and ally bonuses retain their exact scope and are labeled GM-managed, not converted into automatic combat effects.

Audited source corrections repair extraction boundaries and duplicated OCR. Each correction names its printed/PDF page; the original extracted fields remain visible beside the corrected readable result. Unknown notation, unsupported mechanical conditions and unrecognized fatal clauses stay yellow. Green does not certify correctness or complete narrative automation.

Opening review does not edit source JSON or combat state. Tables and combat separately use the same derived corrections. Existing emoji preference changes readable presentation only.
