Critical table review

All critical-strike tables from the published table manifest. Read-only automated checks, not a rules correction or approval workflow.

Green: no automated issues detected. This does NOT mean verified correctness or human approval.

Yellow: needs review. Reasons appear in every flagged cell; color is never the only indicator.

What these checks mean

Unspecified temporary critical durations default to 1 round, including penalties and bonuses. Missing duration alone is not yellow. Explicit counts and relevant permanent/until-healed conditions are preserved; bleeding stays an ongoing rate. Recognized “you gain the initiative” means attacker first next round without a roll. Recognized target death countdowns/instant death use Dies ☠️; ambiguous fatal or inactive-round prose stays yellow. Conditional branches need GM selection. Unknown notation, suspicious OCR, narrative effect text and missing results need source review. These heuristic checks may over-flag or miss prose ambiguities; compare the original source/PDF before deciding.

Review does not edit source text or combat state. Equipment-dependent cells require correctly configured foe equipment; unsupported compound conditions remain yellow. Existing emoji preference affects readable output only.
