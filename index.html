    Miscellaneous information

    Adolescence languages

    Spoken and written are separate skills. Enter total ranks; only ranks above the base shown for each skill spend from this pool.

    Hobby ranks

    Spend ranks on skills or skill categories. A choice may receive only as many ranks as your profession can develop in one level, and its development cost must be below 40.

    No matching ranks. Change the search or filters to show more choices.

    Weapon development costs

    Assign one cost slot to each weapon category. Choosing a slot already in use swaps the two categories. These costs also set the maximum hobby ranks for each category and skill.
