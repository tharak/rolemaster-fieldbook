    Hobby ranks

    Spend ranks on skills or skill categories. A choice may receive only as many ranks as your profession can develop in one level, and its development cost must be below 40.

    Apprenticeship development

    Section 6.0 · One level of development

    —

    Buy ranks with development points. Each skill and category uses its profession cost, and each cost entry allows one rank this level. Ranks from adolescence and hobbies stay separate. Spell lists use T-2.4 costs and count toward the 5-list multiplier in the order you first buy them.

    Weapon DP costs · 6.2

    Assign your profession’s seven weapon cost slots to the weapon categories. Choosing a slot swaps it with its current category.

    Training packages · 6.3

    Choose at most one lifestyle package. Vocational packages may be combined. Package costs share this DP pool.
